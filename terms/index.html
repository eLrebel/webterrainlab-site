The extension

Tiny World creates visual simulations over ordinary webpages. It requires a compatible Chromium-based browser, appropriate site permissions and sufficient device resources. Chrome internal pages and restricted webpages may not support overlays. It does not guarantee identical behavior on every site or every machine.

Free and Premium features

Urban Warfare is the free world. The planned Premium Worlds Pass is a one-time USD $5 purchase granting access to the currently included worlds: Nature, Ocean and Space. It is not a subscription. Applicable sales taxes are calculated at checkout by Paddle.

Future worlds

Future world releases may be free, included in the existing pass, or offered in separate packs. An existing pass does not automatically guarantee every future world; inclusion will be clearly stated when each world launches.

Activation and fair use

Premium access requires a valid key issued following payment confirmation, and the extension periodically verifies that entitlement. An offline grace period may permit continued access when validation is temporarily unavailable. Keys may be revoked following an approved full refund or chargeback. Do not share or resell keys.

Availability and changes

We may update, improve, rebalance or fix worlds as the extension evolves. We will try to preserve paid entitlements during future updates. Performance depends on browser, hardware, webpage complexity and selected world settings.
