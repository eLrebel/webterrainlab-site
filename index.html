THE DETAILS

Built for curiosity.

Is Urban Warfare free?

Yes. Urban Warfare is the free world; the Premium pass covers Nature, Ocean and Space.

Will the extension alter my webpage?

The worlds draw visual overlays and do not rewrite your webpage text. You can disable the animation globally or for individual websites.

Do I need a subscription?

No. The planned Premium Worlds Pass is a one-time $5 payment, with no recurring billing.

Will all future worlds be included?

Not necessarily. Each new world will be labelled as free, included in the existing pass, or part of a separate future pack before release.
