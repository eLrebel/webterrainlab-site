DATA AND PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Draft for merchant review. This page describes the planned implementation and must be verified against the final release and hosting logs before publication as a binding policy.

Last drafted: 11 November 2026. Operator: Aswanth Sreeram · aswanthsreeram@gmail.com.

Extension processing

Tiny World uses webpage geometry to render animated worlds over websites. The game simulation and adaptive behavior settings run in Chrome. The extension's current design does not transmit webpage text, URLs, browsing history or gameplay details to the Paddle checkout or license service.

Game preferences, selected worlds, power state, excluded website hostnames, and local adaptive-memory data are stored in Chrome extension local storage. Chrome may synchronize or back up data according to your own Chrome settings; the extension does not intentionally upload this data to our payment service.

Optional payments

Premium payments, when enabled, will be processed on an approved external checkout website by Paddle. Paddle may process identity, billing, payment, tax and transaction information according to its own privacy practices. We do not collect card numbers inside the Chrome extension.

License validation

The activation service receives your activation key when you verify ownership of Premium Worlds. It stores a one-way hash of that key, an associated Paddle transaction identifier, purchase state and revocation state. The temporary checkout service stores a random purchase-session ID and authorization secret to deliver the activation key after verified payment. Servers and hosting providers may also process network metadata, including IP addresses, for operational security and abuse prevention.

Sharing and retention

Billing data is shared with Paddle to process your purchase. License validation runs on the planned Cloudflare Worker and KV service. The service does not require your page content. License records are retained as needed to verify purchases and process refunds; temporary claim sessions are set to expire after seven days in the current implementation. Actual transaction and hosting retention policies require merchant review before launch.

Your options

Disable the extension at any time; uninstall it to stop animation and remove the extension's local data as Chrome permits. For access, deletion, correction or recovery questions about payment records, email aswanthsreeram@gmail.com. Paddle-related personal data requests may also need to be handled directly by Paddle.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Aswanth Sreeram at aswanthsreeram@gmail.com. This website is not accepting payments.
