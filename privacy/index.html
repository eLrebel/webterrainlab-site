DATA AND PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Sandbox release. Live payments are disabled. Sandbox checkout is available for testing.

Updated: 13 November 2026. Operator: Aswanth Sreeram · aswanthsreeram@gmail.com.

Extension processing

Tiny World uses webpage geometry to render animated worlds over websites. The game simulation and adaptive behavior settings run in Chrome. The extension's current design does not transmit webpage text, URLs, browsing history or gameplay details to the Paddle checkout or license service.

Game preferences, selected worlds, power state, excluded website hostnames, and local adaptive-memory data are stored in Chrome extension local storage. The extension uses local storage, not Chrome sync storage. Device backups may retain copies independently.

Optional payments

Premium payments, when enabled, will be processed on an approved external checkout website by Paddle. Paddle may process identity, billing, payment, tax and transaction information according to its own privacy practices. We do not collect card numbers inside the Chrome extension.

License validation

The activation service receives your activation key when you verify ownership of Premium Worlds. It stores a one-way hash of that key, an associated Paddle transaction identifier, purchase state and revocation state. The checkout service stores a random session ID, a hash of its authorization secret, and the activation key temporarily in readable form so the checkout browser can recover it. Your browser stores the session ID and secret; the extension stores the activation key locally. Servers and hosting providers may also process network metadata, including IP addresses, for operational security and abuse prevention.

Sharing and retention

Billing data is shared with Paddle to process your purchase. License validation runs on Cloudflare Workers and license storage (KV, migrating to D1). The service does not require your page content. License records are retained as needed to verify purchases and process refunds; claim access expires after seven days. The updated service schedules hourly deletion of expired sessions; provider backups may retain copies longer. Browser site data remains until you clear it or the site removes an expired session. Support messages and purchase records may be retained to resolve requests and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Your options

Disable the extension at any time; uninstall it to stop animation and remove the extension's local data as Chrome permits. For access, deletion, correction or recovery questions about payment records, email aswanthsreeram@gmail.com. Paddle-related personal data requests may also need to be handled directly by Paddle.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Aswanth Sreeram at aswanthsreeram@gmail.com. This website is not accepting live payments.
