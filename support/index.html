Getting started

  1. Install the unpacked extension for testing or the public release when available.
  2. Open its popup on an ordinary HTTP/HTTPS webpage, press Power ON, and grant the site permission if requested.
  3. Select Urban Warfare, Nature, Ocean or Space. Only one world runs at a time.
  4. Use the popup's global and per-website controls to pause animations.

Premium activation

When the $5 checkout is live, purchase through the approved Premium checkout. After Paddle confirms payment, the site displays a WTL activation key. Paste it in the extension's Premium section.

Keep the key. If it is lost, payment recovery will require a receipt lookup through the published support channel. The recovery process is not live yet.

Troubleshooting

  • Nothing animates: check Power ON, website permission, and whether this website was disabled in the popup.
  • Outdated world: reload the extension at chrome://extensions, then refresh the webpage.
  • High CPU usage: switch to lower population settings or pause a world on heavy websites.
  • Premium key rejected: verify the spelling and internet connection, then consult the future support contact.
